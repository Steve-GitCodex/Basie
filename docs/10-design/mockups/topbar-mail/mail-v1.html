MAIL — v1 prototypes

Three ways to make Mail quicker to operate. Every frame is interactive with its own inbox: open a mail, claim, use Claim all, delete then Undo, star, and open a battle report (the ⚔ rows). Each option is shown in the desktop modal and in the phone drill-in. Reset restores the inbox. Inputs: docs/research/topbar-mail-*.md. Click a card to select it.

OPTION A — Tabs and a sticky claim bar

The current mail, made faster. A row of tabs (All · Reports · Rewards · System · Starred · Trash) replaces the 160px side rail, and each tab shows unread and claimable badges. Rows have a Claim button, so you don't need to open a mail to collect it. A bottom bar is always in view with Claim all (N), Read all and Delete read. In the reader, the action bar is pinned, Delete is kept apart from Claim, and claiming jumps to the next mail with rewards.

Clearing N reward mails: 2 + 2N clicks today → 2 (dock, Claim all).
Pros: the closest to the current mental model and to the Inventory shell (ADR 0039), so it's the cheapest to build. MailManager already has the bulk methods.
Cons: on desktop there are two panes, so a wide modal is still needed. Archive moves into Trash and Starred, so there's one fewer bucket to think about.
Delete read skips starred and unclaimed mail and can be undone, so nothing valuable is ever lost.

OPTION B — Category hub (genre style) · CHOSEN (ADR 0040)

The Last Shelter / Whiteout pattern. You start on large category tiles, each showing its unread and gift counts and its own Read & claim all. Inside a category, mails open in place as an expanding row instead of a separate reader pane. Desktop keeps the tiles on the left. On a phone you go hub → list, and that's as deep as it gets.

Clearing a category: 2 clicks (dock, tile's claim all). Clearing everything: dock and the header's Claim all.
Pros: feels like the genre. The rows that expand in place mean there is no reader pane to go back and forth from, and the list position stays where it was. It also works well in a narrow modal.
Cons: long battle reports make the list very tall. Searching and a single list of everything are harder because they're one level down.

OPTION C — Single feed and reward tray

There are no categories, just one chronological feed with filter chips (All · Unread · Rewards · Reports · Starred). A reward tray at the top adds up everything waiting to be claimed, with a single Claim all. Select adds checkboxes and a bulk bar (Read · Star · Delete). Mails open as a sheet that slides over the feed.

Pros: the tray shows exactly what you'll get before you tap, which is the most satisfying moment in Mail. The sheet keeps the feed scroll intact. Select mode covers every bulk case and has a tap alternative to swiping (WCAG 2.5.1).
Cons: the biggest departure from the Inventory shell. Filter chips are less discoverable than tabs for Trash and archive.
Later: swipe-to-delete on phones can sit on top of select mode.
