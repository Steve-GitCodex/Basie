HERO LEVEL-UP — v1 prototype

XP items are no longer used from the Inventory. They are spent from a hero's own page, so a roster of 30 heroes never means picking from a list. The numbers use the real items (Tomes 250/1K/5K, XP cards 500/2.5K/12K, fragments 50) and the real hero curve (100 + 20·(L−1)) × tier. This one is Epic (×1.25), Lv 21, cap Lv 40. Try the steppers, Next level and Fill to cap.

1 · Hero detail → Level up sheet

Entry: a Level up button next to the XP bar. It replaces the inline Tome buttons.
Sheet: every owned XP item with its value and a stepper. The preview shows "Lv 21 → 24" on the bar, plus the XP left over.
Next level and Fill to cap fill the steppers with the least waste: large items first without going over, then the smallest item to finish.
Fragments are listed separately and never auto-picked, because they are also the awakening and summon currency.
Over the cap: the preview warns how much XP would be wasted. Items come in fixed sizes, so Fill to cap can overshoot by at most one smallest item. The warning shows the exact amount, for example "150 XP would be wasted", and you can step it down.
Phone: a bottom sheet. Desktop: a panel over the info column.

2 · Inventory: XP item detail (no hero picker)

XP tomes and cards show what they do and how many you own, plus Level a hero ›, which opens the Heroes roster.
A hero fragment shows Open Aldric Cross ›, which goes straight to that hero's page.
The Trading Post's "Use" on these items routes the same way.
