The HUD badge opens a Buffs side panel (same slide-in shell as Inventory). Active lists everything with a clock. Overview lists every stat, with each source's contribution and the real compounded total, so the number matches the resource bar.

One list, every clock. Item boosts (BuffManager), ruin buffs (WorldMapManager timed) and event modifiers (EventManager) all come through the ledger. The source tag says where each came from.
Idle slots = buy-and-use (State of Survival pattern). Only stats with an item in the catalogue get a slot. Today that's production, so gather and attack slots appear once those items exist.
Timers patch in place through TimerService. No re-render on each tick (fixes the frozen countdown).
Expiry: a toast, and the row fades out. World timed buffs get that too; today they expire silently.

Effective total = the real pipeline math: add within a layer, then multiply across layers. The footer line shows the multiplication, so "+50% and +15% isn't +65%" is explained right on screen.
Timed sources carry their clock (amber), so you see what's about to fall off.
Zero stats stay listed (dim "—"). They show what can be boosted and give you a reason to take regions and do research.
Resource chips in the HUD get a hover tooltip that is this same breakdown (fills the "no rate breakdown" gap).
